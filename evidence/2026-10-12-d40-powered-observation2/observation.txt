ME3630 D40 second powered-dock observation
Date: 2026-10-12 (Asia/Shanghai)

The user replaced the externally powered dock/hub and kept the module in the
WM6 x+n composition. No software mode change, WM6 reboot, SIM/APN change, or
module reset was issued.

After a 15 second settling wait, probe_active.ps1 -UsbState reported
ACTIVE_ME3630_PORT_BLOCKS=0. The exact 19d2:1432 mapping still pointed to
ME3630ComV18D40.dll. A fresh collect_network_state.ps1 pull showed the D40
accumulated driver-log event count remained 151; no new ATTACH appeared after
the previous observation and the last recorded events were still the earlier
DETACH/USB error sequence.

No radio probe or RAS attempt was started because this dock condition produced
no new ME3630 USB attach and therefore no COM1/COM5 endpoint to test. This
second dock does not prove its external supply is ineffective; it shows that
WM6's OHCI host still did not establish a downstream ME3630 data enumeration.
The next useful setup is a direct data path to the carrier/module with a
carrier-supported external supply, or a hub explicitly known to work with this
WM6 USB host and to preserve the required upstream VBUS detection. Do not feed
raw 5 V to ME3630 V_MAIN.
